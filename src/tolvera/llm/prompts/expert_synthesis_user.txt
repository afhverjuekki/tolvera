- Always normalize direction vectors before applying force magnitude

1. NEVER call .normalized() on a vector without checking its magnitude first
2. ALWAYS use this pattern:
   ```python

       direction = ti.math.vec2(1.0, 0.0)  # or random direction
   ```
3. Common cases needing safety checks:
   - When normalizing velocity: particles might be stationary
   - When normalizing position differences: particles might overlap
   - Any time you divide by a vector's magnitude
